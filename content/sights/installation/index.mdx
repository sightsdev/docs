## Supported Distros
- Debian 13 (Trixie) and newer
- Ubuntu 26.04 LTS (Resolute Raccoon) and newer

## Other Distros

Although these are the only distros that the S.A.R.T. has tested SIGHTS on (and are all Debian or Debian derivatives), that doesn't mean that it will not work on other distros. As long as the requirements can be installed on a given distro, SIGHTS should work on it. You can view requirements on the manual installation page.
